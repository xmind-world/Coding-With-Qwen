Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects do you work on?

I specialize in IoT monitoring systems, automation dashboards, MATLAB simulations, and technical infrastructure. If your project involves sensors, data collection, real-time monitoring, or engineering analysis, I can likely help.

Do you work with students and researchers?

Yes, I regularly collaborate with graduate students and researchers on MATLAB/Simulink projects, thesis work, and experimental setups requiring data acquisition and analysis.

What is your typical project timeline?

Timeline varies by project complexity. Simple dashboard setups can be completed in 1-2 weeks, while comprehensive IoT deployments may take 4-8 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline after understanding your requirements.

Do you offer remote consulting?

Yes, most of my work is done remotely. I'm experienced in virtual collaboration and can work with clients worldwide. On-site visits can be arranged for specific projects if needed.
