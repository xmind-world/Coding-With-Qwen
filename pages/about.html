About Mohammad

Technical Consultant & IoT Solution Builder

Professional Summary

I help engineers, researchers, and businesses transform technical ideas into practical, working systems. My focus is on delivering measurable results through IoT monitoring solutions, automation dashboards, and engineering consulting.

Rather than simply implementing technologies, I work closely with clients to understand their core challenges and design solutions that address real operational needs. Whether it's collecting sensor data, building real-time dashboards, or deploying scalable infrastructure, my approach combines technical expertise with practical problem-solving.

My work spans across multiple domains: from academic research projects requiring precise MATLAB simulations, to industrial IoT deployments needing robust, 24/7 monitoring capabilities. Each project is approached with the same commitment to quality, reliability, and clear communication.

My Approach

Problem-First Thinking

I start by understanding your actual challenge, not by pushing specific technologies. The right solution emerges from deep analysis of requirements and constraints.

Practical Implementation

Theoretical knowledge is valuable, but working systems are what matter. I focus on solutions that are maintainable, scalable, and actually solve your problem.

Collaborative Process

You know your domain best. I bring technical expertise. Together, we create solutions that leverage both perspectives for optimal results.

Technical Background

My expertise spans electrical engineering fundamentals, embedded systems programming, and modern web-based dashboard development. This combination allows me to bridge the gap between hardware-level sensor integration and high-level data visualization.

Core competencies include:

  • MATLAB & Simulink Engineering simulations, control system design, signal processing
  • ESP32 & Embedded Systems Sensor integration, wireless communication, low-power design
  • Node-RED Flow-based programming, MQTT brokers, custom dashboard development
  • Docker & Infrastructure Containerization, deployment automation, service orchestration
  • Data Visualization Grafana, real-time dashboards, time-series databases

Career Focus
